The treasury

Every SPORES you earn is a claim on HYPE sitting in the Liquidity Spores contract. You can redeem it for that HYPE at any time, or spend it on fertiliser and strain unlocks — which burns it, leaves the HYPE behind, and raises the floor for everyone still holding.

Anyone can drip the treasury

Once a week, 2% of the reserve moves into backing and mints the matching SPORES to the farm. It is permissionless, so the cycle does not depend on us being awake. If it is overdue, call it yourself.

This replaced the auto-compounding vault. Under the old design the farm minted rewards on a schedule and a vault re-staked them for you. Rewards now come from SHROOMS trading fees, which arrive in lumps, so there is nothing to continuously compound — there is a treasury that holds the fees and a farm that streams them out.
