Two tokens, one loop

SHROOMS — the key

The token you stake. Launched on Montra with the whole supply locked as liquidity, so there is no team wallet and no unlock cliff. Every strain in the grove takes SHROOMS, or the SHROOMS-HYPE LP — nothing else is accepted.

Trading it produces the fees that fund every reward paid here. That is the entire funding model: no emissions, no inflation, no treasury handing out its own token.

SPORES — the harvest

What you earn. Minted only against HYPE the treasury has already received, so it cannot be printed on a schedule and supply is an output of real activity.

Redeem it for HYPE at the floor, or spend it in the grove: fertilise a plot for +0.50x, or burn it to unlock a Legendary strain. Spending burns it and leaves the HYPE behind, which lifts the floor for everyone still holding.

Eight strains, four rarities

Every strain pays SPORES out of the same fixed harvest, split by weight. Legendary strains draw the largest share and cost the most SPORES to unlock. LP strains carry 60% of all weight between them, because liquidity depth is what generates the fees in the first place.
